Configure your game world

The game world configurations exist in two directories - ServerFolder/Data and ServerFolder/GameServer/Data.

In your game world, you can configure:

  • Monsters: Stats, Spawn points
  • Invasions and events: Rate, amount, rewards
  • Items: Stats, drop rate, drop groups
  • Characters: Stats, skills, PvP balance, experience rate

This section guides you through how the configurations work and how to change them.
